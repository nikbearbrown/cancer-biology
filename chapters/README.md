# Cancer Biology: chapter guide

[Return to the book overview](../README.md).

### Part I · Cells, cancer and risk

1. [The Building Blocks of Life: Normal Cell Biology](../chapters/01-the-building-blocks-of-life-normal-cell-biology.md): Review normal cell components and regulation as a baseline for disease.
2. [Introduction to Cancer: A Disease of Deregulation](../chapters/02-introduction-to-cancer-a-disease-of-deregulation.md): Explain how deregulated cell behavior differs from normal tissue control.
3. [Cancer Epidemiology and Risk Factors](../chapters/03-cancer-epidemiology-and-risk-factors.md): Distinguish population risk patterns from causes in an individual.

### Part II · Genetics and gene regulation

4. [Genetics and Genomic Instability in Cancer](../chapters/04-genetics-and-genomic-instability-in-cancer.md): Connect altered genomes and instability to changes in cell behavior.
5. [Oncogenes](../chapters/05-oncogenes.md): Explain how a growth-promoting pathway can become persistently active.
6. [Tumor Suppressor Genes](../chapters/06-tumor-suppressor-genes.md): Explain how loss of growth controls can contribute to cancer.
7. [Epigenetics in Cancer: The Methylation and Histone Code](../chapters/07-epigenetics-in-cancer-the-methylation-and-histone-code.md): Distinguish DNA methylation and histone regulation from DNA sequence change.
8. [Epigenetics in Cancer: Noncoding RNAs, Reprogramming, and Therapy](../chapters/08-epigenetics-in-cancer-noncoding-rnas-reprogramming-and-therapy.md): Describe roles of noncoding RNA and gene regulation in cancer at a conceptual level.

### Part III · Causes, division and cell death

9. [Cancer Etiology: Chemical and Radiation Carcinogens](../chapters/09-cancer-etiology-chemical-and-radiation-carcinogens.md): Interpret evidence about chemical and radiation exposures and cancer risk.
10. [Cancer Etiology: Viral and Bacterial Carcinogens](../chapters/10-cancer-etiology-viral-and-bacterial-carcinogens.md): Describe evidence that some infections are associated with cancer risk.
11. [Cell Cycle Control and Cancer: The Cycle and Its Checkpoints](../chapters/11-cell-cycle-control-and-cancer-the-cycle-and-its-checkpoints.md): Describe checkpoints that ordinarily regulate progression through the cell cycle.
12. [Cell Cycle Control and Cancer: Disruption and Therapy](../chapters/12-cell-cycle-control-and-cancer-disruption-and-therapy.md): Connect a disrupted checkpoint to disease mechanisms and broad treatment concepts.
13. [Apoptosis in Cancer: The Death Programs](../chapters/13-apoptosis-in-cancer-the-death-programs.md): Compare major programmed cell-death pathways and their normal roles.
14. [Apoptosis in Cancer: Evasion and Restoration](../chapters/14-apoptosis-in-cancer-evasion-and-restoration.md): Explain how tumors can evade cell death and why this matters conceptually for treatment.

### Part IV · Metabolism and tumor surroundings

15. [Cancer Metabolism: The Warburg Effect and Glucose](../chapters/15-cancer-metabolism-the-warburg-effect-and-glucose.md): Distinguish altered glucose use from a one-cause explanation of a tumor.
16. [Cancer Metabolism: Lipids, Amino Acids, and the Tumor Microenvironment](../chapters/16-cancer-metabolism-lipids-amino-acids-and-the-tumor-microenvironment.md): Connect nutrient use and the tumor environment to cell behavior.
